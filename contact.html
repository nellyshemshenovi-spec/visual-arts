About & Inquiries

Nelly Shemshenovi

Member of the International Creative Union of Artists of Georgia

Tbilisi-based visual artist and fine art photographer specializing in atmospheric botanical noir, surreal dreamscapes, and evocative natural landscapes. Her artistic practice merges classical photographic sensibilities with modern digital darkroom techniques.

Through subtle manipulation of twilight hues, rich grain, and velvety shadows, Shemshenovi transforms ordinary botanical forms and serene seascapes into contemplative spaces. Her works explore themes of solitude, fragile resilience, and the unspoken poetry found in silent hours.
